The planned system brings essential donor coordination activities into one secure place.

♙01 / DONOR RECORDS

Donor registration

Maintain donor contact details, blood group, consent and availability status with controlled access.

⌕02 / SEARCH

Find potential donors

Authorized staff can search records by blood group and location, then review donor availability.

✚03 / EMERGENCY

Urgent blood requests

Record a hospital request and track its status from creation through follow-up.

↗04 / NOTIFICATIONS

SMS alert workflow

Plan urgent SMS notifications to consenting donors after staff verification and approval.
