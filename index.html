HOW IT WORKS03 / A CLEARER PROCESS

From request
to response.

A proposed four-step journey designed to support hospital teams. It does not replace medical assessment or professional judgement.

01Register

Consenting donors provide their details and blood group.

02Review

Authorised staff review an emergency request and search records.

03Notify

Approved SMS alerts are sent to appropriate consenting donors.

04Coordinate

Hospital staff follow up and assess donor suitability clinically.

RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN04 / TRUST & SAFETY

People first.
Always.

Blood donation is a healthcare matter. The system is intended to support communication—not make medical decisions. Real-world use requires institutional approval, secure implementation and clinical oversight.

01

Informed consent

Donors should understand and agree to how their details are stored and when they may be contacted.

02

Privacy and access control

Personal information should be protected and visible only to authorised users with a legitimate role.

03

Clinical decision-making

Qualified healthcare professionals—not software—determine donor eligibility and suitability.
